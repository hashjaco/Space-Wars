# AI-Generated Music Resources for Space Case

## Overview

This document outlines affordable AI music generation resources suitable for game development. These tools can generate background music, sound effects, and ambient tracks for Space Case.

## Recommended Services

### 1. **Mubert** ⭐ Recommended
- **Pricing:** Free tier available, Pro starts at $14/month
- **Features:**
  - Real-time AI music generation
  - Royalty-free music
  - Customizable styles and moods
  - API access for integration
- **Best For:** Background music, ambient tracks
- **Website:** https://mubert.com
- **Notes:** Excellent for space-themed ambient music

### 2. **AIVA (Artificial Intelligence Virtual Artist)**
- **Pricing:** Free tier (limited), Creative plan at $11/month
- **Features:**
  - Professional-quality compositions
  - Multiple genres and styles
  - Commercial licensing included
  - High-quality exports
- **Best For:** Epic boss battle music, cinematic tracks
- **Website:** https://www.aiva.ai
- **Notes:** Great for dramatic, orchestral space themes

### 3. **Boomy**
- **Pricing:** Free tier available, Pro at $9.99/month
- **Features:**
  - Quick music generation (30 seconds)
  - Multiple genres
  - Simple interface
  - Royalty-free downloads
- **Best For:** Quick background tracks, testing
- **Website:** https://boomy.com
- **Notes:** Good for rapid prototyping

### 4. **Soundful**
- **Pricing:** Free tier (watermarked), Premium at $29.99/month
- **Features:**
  - High-quality AI music
  - Genre-specific templates
  - Royalty-free commercial use
  - Multiple export formats
- **Best For:** Professional game music
- **Website:** https://soundful.com
- **Notes:** Slightly more expensive but high quality

### 5. **Amper Music (Now Shutterstock Audio)**
- **Pricing:** Pay-per-track model, ~$0.99-$4.99 per track
- **Features:**
  - Customizable tracks
  - Professional quality
  - Commercial licensing
- **Best For:** One-off tracks, specific needs
- **Website:** https://www.shutterstock.com/audio
- **Notes:** Good for specific tracks without subscription

### 6. **Ecrett Music**
- **Pricing:** Free tier available, Pro at $9.99/month
- **Features:**
  - Scene-based music generation
  - Multiple styles
  - Royalty-free
  - Easy customization
- **Best For:** Scene-specific music
- **Website:** https://ecrettmusic.com
- **Notes:** Good for matching music to game scenes

## Recommended Approach for Space Case

### Phase 1: Prototyping (Free Tier)
- Use **Mubert** or **Boomy** free tiers for initial tracks
- Generate 3-5 background tracks
- Test in-game to see what works

### Phase 2: Production (Paid Tier)
- **Mubert Pro** ($14/month) for:
  - Main menu music
  - Background gameplay music
  - Ambient space tracks
  
- **AIVA Creative** ($11/month) for:
  - Boss battle music
  - Level completion themes
  - Epic cinematic moments

### Phase 3: Polish (One-time purchases)
- Use **Shutterstock Audio** for specific tracks that need refinement
- Purchase individual tracks for key moments

## Music Style Recommendations

### Background Music
- **Style:** Ambient electronic, space synth
- **Mood:** Mysterious, adventurous
- **Tempo:** Moderate (90-120 BPM)
- **Tools:** Mubert, Ecrett Music

### Boss Battles
- **Style:** Epic orchestral, intense electronic
- **Mood:** Dramatic, intense
- **Tempo:** Fast (140-160 BPM)
- **Tools:** AIVA, Soundful

### Menu Music
- **Style:** Calm ambient, space-themed
- **Mood:** Peaceful but mysterious
- **Tempo:** Slow to moderate (60-100 BPM)
- **Tools:** Mubert, Boomy

### Victory/Completion
- **Style:** Triumphant, uplifting
- **Mood:** Celebratory
- **Tempo:** Moderate to fast (100-130 BPM)
- **Tools:** AIVA, Soundful

## Implementation Tips

1. **Loop Seamlessly:** Ensure tracks loop without noticeable breaks
2. **Volume Levels:** Normalize all tracks to similar volume levels
3. **Format:** Use compressed formats (MP3/AAC) for iOS, but keep high-quality originals
4. **Fade In/Out:** Add fade transitions for smooth music changes
5. **Layering:** Consider layering tracks for dynamic music (calm + intense layers)

## Legal Considerations

- **Royalty-Free:** All listed services provide royalty-free music
- **Commercial Use:** Most paid tiers include commercial licensing
- **Attribution:** Check individual service terms (usually not required)
- **Exclusivity:** Most tracks are non-exclusive (others can use same tracks)

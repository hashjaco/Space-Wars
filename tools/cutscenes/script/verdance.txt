# @pause S, @sfx name [+/-offset] [gain], SPEAKER[ (radio)]: line  -- see mix.py

@sfx cave-drip -1.6 0.8
@sfx comms-open -0.3

@shot 1 action: Steam and spores drift through the green-lit cave; the dead beast's necks settle as the small fighter hovers, engines glowing.

@shot 2 talk: CASE portrait=case-cockpit

@sfx tags-jingle -0.2

@shot 3 action: The gloved hand slowly lifts the dripping tangle of dog tags out of the nest; slime strings and drips, tags sway and glint.

@shot 4 talk: HOLLIS portrait=hollis-console
@pause 1.2
HOLLIS (radio): Read me a name.

@shot 5 talk: CASE portrait=case-nest
CASE: Hollis, there's too many to--

@shot 6 talk: HOLLIS portrait=hollis-console
HOLLIS (radio): Read me one.

@shot 7 talk: CASE portrait=case-nest

@shot 8 talk: HOLLIS portrait=hollis-console
@pause 1.4

@sfx beacon-tick -0.4

@shot 9 action: The pilot holds up the blinking orange beacon; its light pulses in a slow tick as she turns it in her hand, green cave light around her.
CASE: There's something else. A beacon, still blinking. Not one of ours.

@shot 10 talk: HOLLIS portrait=hollis-console
HOLLIS (radio): Orange casing. Three fins. Ticks like a cheap watch?

@shot 11 talk: CASE portrait=case-nest
CASE: You've seen one.
@sfx holo-flicker -0.3

@shot 12 action: A flickering holographic Militia dossier of the smuggler rotates above the beacon, scan lines rolling, glitches crackling.
HOLLIS (radio): That's Vaunt's hardware. A smuggler who calls himself a baron. He'll sell anything to anyone, and he charges extra for the anyone.

@shot 13 action: Slow push toward the molten forge world as smokestacks belch ash into space and lava rivers glow.
HOLLIS (radio): The beacon's pointing at Ashfall. His forges.
CASE (radio): Great. I love a man with forges.

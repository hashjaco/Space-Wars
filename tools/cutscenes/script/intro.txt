# INTRO -- "Lights Out". Kestrel Station, the night the galaxies went dark.
# @shot N action: <motion>  |  @shot N talk: SPEAKER portrait=<name>  |  @shot N still
# @pause S, @sfx name [+/-offset] [gain], SPEAKER[ (radio)]: line  -- see mix.py

@sfx galaxy-hum -1.6 0.9

@shot 1 action: Slow push in as the five galaxies gutter out one by one, the nearest fading to black.
@sfx comms-open -0.3
HOLLIS (radio): Militia net, this is Hollis at Kestrel Station. Anyone still out there, sound off.

@shot 2 talk: HOLLIS portrait=hollis-console
@pause 1.4
HOLLIS (radio): ...Anyone.

@shot 3 action: Slow drift past the frozen derelict fleet tumbling in the dark; the faint violet ring pulses far behind.
HOLLIS (radio): Five galaxies went dark in one night. Colonies. Beacons. The whole Third Fleet. They didn't scatter. Didn't call for help. They just... went out.
@sfx hangar-amb -0.5 0.8

@shot 4 action: Slow dolly across the dim hangar toward the last green fighter on its lit pad; lamps sway, dust drifts.
HOLLIS: And I've got one ship left that still flies. Barely.

@shot 5 talk: CASE portrait=case-hangar
CASE: Then it's a good thing I only need one.

@shot 6 talk: HOLLIS portrait=hollis-hangar
HOLLIS: Case. You're grounded. You are always grounded.

@shot 7 talk: CASE portrait=case-hangar
CASE: And yet here I am, standing next to the only thing in five galaxies with its lights on.

@shot 8 talk: HOLLIS portrait=hollis-hangar
HOLLIS: That's the cargo hauler's reading lamp.

@shot 9 talk: CASE portrait=case-hangar
CASE: Then I'll fly very carefully.

@shot 10 talk: HOLLIS portrait=hollis-hangar
HOLLIS: Whatever's eating the light is still out there, and it's still hungry. You see it, you turn around.

@shot 11 talk: CASE portrait=case-hangar
CASE: Sure.

@shot 12 talk: HOLLIS portrait=hollis-hangar
HOLLIS: Case.

@shot 13 talk: CASE portrait=case-cockpit
CASE: I'll turn around and look at it. Very hard.
@sfx engine-launch -0.8

@shot 14 action: The fighter's engines flare bright blue and it accelerates away toward the green planet, shrinking into the distance.
HOLLIS (radio): First stop, Verdance. Last signal came from the jungle world. Bring back answers. Bring back my ship.
CASE (radio): In that order?
@pause 0.8
HOLLIS (radio): Any order. Just come back.
